# Task A: beat rate of a paced iPSC-cardiomyocyte microbundle

The data is one unmodified brightfield TIFF stack (Ex00628.tif, 150 frames, 512 x 512) of a hiPSC-derived cardiac microbundle on a two-pillar strain-gauge platform, from the CC0 Chen/Lejeune lab deposit (Dryad 10.5061/dryad.sqv9s4nbg, Zenodo 11120809). The depositors document this condition as recorded at day 7 under 1 Hz electrical stimulation, so the ground truth is 60 bpm independent of any analysis. The prompt does not mention pacing. ground_truth.py confirms it from the data: displacement from a relaxed reference frame peaks once per beat, and the inter-beat intervals from the TIFF's per-plane timestamps are 0.998, 1.003, 1.002 and 0.995 s (60.03 bpm, CV 0.32%). The same method gives 59.80 to 60.27 bpm on all eight tissues in this condition that follow the stimulus (Ex00628 to Ex00635).

**Traps, each measured on this file:**
- **Two motion peaks per beat.** Consecutive frame differencing and optical-flow speed each show a contraction peak and a smaller relaxation peak about 0.33 s later. Counting all peaks gives 119.3 bpm; the mean interval between them gives 120.6 bpm.
- **Harmonic in the spectrum.** The tallest peak in the frame-difference spectrum is at 3.00 Hz (third harmonic, amplitude 1.00 against 0.98 at the 1.00 Hz fundamental), giving 180.1 bpm. The optical-flow speed spectrum peaks at the fundamental here, so this one catches frame differencing only.
- **Nominal frame rate.** The metadata sheet says 30 Hz, which the prompt quotes. The per-plane timestamps give 32.91 frames/s, consistent with the 30 ms exposure in frame-transfer mode. Any correct method run at 30 frames/s returns 54.5 to 54.7 bpm. Eight paced tissues reading 9% below their stimulus rate under the 30 Hz assumption is what rules that rate out.
- **Mid-beat start.** The recording opens partway through a contraction. Five displacement peaks over 4.527 s give 66.3 bpm and four complete beats give 53.0 bpm; the mean inter-beat interval avoids both.

**Tolerance.** 60 +/- 3 bpm. It absorbs one-frame quantization of peak times (about 1.8 bpm) and rejects the nearest trap (54.5 bpm).

**Limitations.** One false pass exists: counting five peaks and dividing by a 5.0 s duration computed from 30 fps lands on 60.0 bpm because the two errors cancel. The recording holds only about 4.5 beats, which limits how much rhythm analysis it supports, though it is enough for rate. Three other tissues in the same condition (Ex00636 to Ex00638) do not follow the 1 Hz stimulus cleanly (57.3, 62.2 and 50.0 bpm, CV 2.9 to 9.5%); they were not used, though they would suit a harder capture or arrhythmia variant. The 30 Hz versus 32.9 frames/s discrepancy is inferred from the timestamps and the pacing together. No statement from the depositors explains it. The likeliest explanation is that the 30 ms exposure was transcribed as 30 Hz, but that is my inference.

**Model results** (autonomous agents, three runs each). Claude Haiku, Sonnet and Opus passed 9 of 9, each reading the per-frame timestamps. GPT-6 Luna passed 1 of 3, answering 56.0 and 54.5 bpm in the other two; Gemini 3 Flash's one completed run answered 54.5 bpm. Both took the sheet's 30 Hz at face value.
