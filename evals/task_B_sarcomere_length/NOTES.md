# Task B notes

The data are one well-organized day-32 hiPSC-cardiomyocyte from the Allen Institute ACTN2-mEGFP dataset (Gerbin et al. 2021): a raw 50-plane spinning-disk z-stack cropped around the cell, voxel size 0.1195 x 0.1195 x 0.33 um carried in the OME-XML, plus the authors' mask for the cell. The ground truth is 1.72 um, the mean of a whole-cell 2D power spectrum radial peak (1.685 um) and orientation-aligned z-line profiles (1.746 um; interval mode 1.724 um), with a tile-wise 2D FFT agreeing at 1.679 um and the authors' own per-cell value at 1.80 um (whole-pixel GLCM). The trap that fires is orientation: the myofibrils run about 44 degrees from the x axis, so axis-aligned methods read long, at 1.91 um (1D autocorrelation along x or y), 2.02 and 2.29 um (1D spectra along x and y) and 2.39 um (line scan along x or y). Reporting pixels gives 14.1, and taking the spectral maximum without a high-pass or band limit gives cell-scale periods of 14.8 or 37.7 um. Three proposed traps don't fire in this cell and are listed as passing in the eval: the defocused middle plane (1.634 um), the unmasked crop (1.685 um) and the nominal 0.1243 um pixel size (1.752 um). The second harmonic is weak (7% of the fundamental) and misleads only an agent searching below 1.2 um (0.865 um). Tolerance is +/-0.12 um (one pixel), window [1.60, 1.84]. The two ground-truth methods differ by 0.06 um (half a pixel), probably because straight 12 um profiles cross gently curving myofibrils slightly obliquely and the peak-interval distribution has a long upper tail from missed z-lines. The tolerance covers that gap, though the margin to the nearest failing answer is 0.07 um. The Allen terms permit noncommercial redistribution only, so placing the snapshot in a commercial benchmark would need Allen's written permission. Model results (autonomous agents, three runs each): Claude Haiku, Sonnet and Opus passed 9 of 9; GPT-6 Luna passed 2 of 3, with one run at 1.85 um.
